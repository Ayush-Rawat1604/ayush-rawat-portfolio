Project Overview

This project demonstrates a complete V6 engine assembly workflow in SolidWorks. The design brings together major engine components and focuses on accurate assembly relationships, mechanical mates and clear engineering presentation.

Hands-on work included the crankshaft, pistons, connecting rods, camshaft, timing mechanism, engine block, cylinder head, rocker arm, oil pan, front cover and air filter components.
