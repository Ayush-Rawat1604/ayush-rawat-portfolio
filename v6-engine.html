PROJECT 01 / FLAGSHIP

V6 Engine Assembly

A detailed V6 engine assembly developed in SOLIDWORKS to strengthen advanced part modelling, assembly design, mechanism understanding and visual presentation.

← Back to ProjectsView Motion Study ↗

Objective

Develop a complete V6 engine CAD assembly to practice complex mechanical component modelling, assembly relationships and presentation of a multi-component mechanism.

Software

SolidWorksAssembly DesignMotion Study3D CAD

Work demonstrated

  • Individual mechanical component modelling
  • Multi-component assembly
  • Assembly mates and positioning
  • Mechanism/motion study
  • Multiple presentation views
  • Visual inspection of the assembly

Engineering learning

Improved understanding of design intent, component relationships, assembly structure and communicating a complex mechanical system through CAD visuals.

MOTION

Assembly Motion

Motion study supplied from the SolidWorks assembly. Any performance values or dimensional specifications should be added only after they are verified from the CAD model.

NEXT ITERATION

How I would take this project further

  • Create production-ready part drawings with dimensions and tolerances.
  • Add an assembly drawing with BOM and balloons.
  • Apply appropriate GD&T to critical interfaces.
  • Document materials and manufacturing processes for selected components.
  • Validate critical moving interfaces using interference and clearance checks.
