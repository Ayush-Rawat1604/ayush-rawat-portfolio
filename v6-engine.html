Project Overview

The V6 Engine Assembly is the flagship portfolio project, designed to demonstrate the ability to break a complex mechanical system into individual components and bring those components together into a structured assembly.

The portfolio presentation emphasizes modelling discipline, assembly structure, component relationships and clear technical communication.
